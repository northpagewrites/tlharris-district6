## Priorities & Progress

No external source was added for this page. That is the point worth recording:
the eight priority subjects are seeded from the 2026 campaign platform as
*subjects to track*, and carry no baseline, target, target date, action or
evidence, because none of those exist from a verified public source.

Two things are needed before any figure appears on a priority:

1. **The original campaign wording**, verbatim, from the archived campaign site
   or the campaign's own records. It is not in this repository. It must not be
   written from memory. Once supplied it is stored in `historical_target` and
   renders under a label marking it as historical context, never as a current
   commitment.
2. **A published baseline**, from the district, the Tennessee Department of
   Education, or another official source, named and dated. Until then each
   priority states "Baseline not yet established from a verified public source."
   and no progress figure is drawn.

The only statement about Board composition used on these pages — that the Board
comprises nine elected members — comes from S1.
