About

T. L. Harris

T. L. Harris represents District 6 on the Memphis-Shelby County Schools Board of Education.

Background

Board Member Harris is a graduate of East High School and continued his education at LeMoyne-Owen College, Louisiana State University, and the University of Memphis.

His career spans public service and leadership: he is a military veteran, has worked with the Memphis Police Department, held C-suite executive roles, worked in case management with Youth Villages, served in youth ministry, and worked as a CFO for a private medical office. He has also worked in music production, songwriting, and radio.

Why he serves

Board Member Harris's connection to community service is personal: he once received support from the Neighborhood Christian Center and later returned to serve the organization himself.

He describes himself as an advocate for second chances, believing that a person's past should not determine their future, and that strong communities are built through mentorship, resources, accountability, education, and real opportunity.

What he brings to the Board

Harris's varied background in finance, executive leadership, public safety, youth advocacy, and communication informs how he approaches decisions on the Board, with a focus on strengthening families, empowering young people, managing resources responsibly, and creating opportunities for the community.
