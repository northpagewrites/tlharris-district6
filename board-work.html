Board Work

Follow the meetings, issues, documents and public business of the Memphis-Shelby County Schools Board of Education, and see the schools T. L. Harris represents as District 6's member.

What the Board does

The Board of Education governs the business operations of Memphis-Shelby County Schools and is committed to its mission of preparing all students for success in learning, leadership and life.

Composition

Nine elected members

One member is elected per district across Shelby County, including District 6.

Authority

Governs as a body

The Board sets policy and governs MSCS's business operations collectively — no single member acts alone.

Cadence

Meets monthly

Regular Business Meetings fall on the final Tuesday of each month at 5:30 p.m., with a Work Session the Tuesday before.

Access

Open to the public

Meetings are open to residents, streamed live online, and broadcast on Cable Channel 19 and Radio 88.5 FM.
